Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Team NEXORA and NEC 2026.

What is Team NEXORA & NEC 2026?
NEXORA is the official team representing BBS College of Engineering & Technology (BBSCET) in the National Entrepreneurship Challenge (NEC 2026) organized by E-Cell IIT Bombay.
Who can participate in the challenge tracks?
All engineering, management, and diploma students from BBSCET and affiliated colleges can register teams (2 to 4 members) in any of the challenge tracks.
What format is required for Pitch Deck submissions?
Submissions should be in PDF format (maximum 8-10 slides) covering Problem Statement, Solution/MVP, Market Size, Revenue Model, and Team Overview.
Who manages the technical portal & platform?
The platform is designed & managed by Prajwal Sharma (Tech & Web Lead) and Ayush Tripathi (Web Design Team) under Team NEXORA.
